The Kidane Dynasty

About the Series

The fate of a kingdom rests in the hands of six extraordinary heirs.

Born into a royal bloodline blessed with elemental powers, the members of the Kidane Dynasty must navigate political intrigue, dangerous enemies, powerful magic, and life-changing bonds as they fight to protect their family, their kingdom, and their future.

Each novel follows a different heir on their journey through love, war, destiny, and the burdens of power, while a larger story unfolds that threatens the very foundation of the throne itself.
